# Privacy Notice & Biometric Data Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026  
**Project:** Signo (Greek Sign Language AI Learning Platform)

Signo is committed to protecting user privacy, biometric security, and camera stream data integrity. This document outlines our data privacy practices in compliance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU 2016/679)**, **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)**, and **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**.

---

## 1. Zero Server-Side Transmission (100% Client-Side Processing)
- **Webcam Feed Non-Retention:** Your video stream is accessed strictly via standard HTML5 browser APIs (`navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia`). Video frames are rendered exclusively into local DOM `<video>` and `<canvas>` memory structures.
- **No Video Data Streaming:** Video frames **NEVER** leave your physical device. No video frames, screenshots, or camera streams are transmitted over network sockets, HTTP endpoints, or cloud servers.
- **Biometric Coordinates Local Inference:** 3D hand landmark coordinates (21 spatial points) generated by Google MediaPipe are processed entirely within in-memory Web Workers and browser WebGL contexts. Coordinates are discarded immediately after frame evaluation and are never logged, stored on disk, or sent to telemetry servers.
- **Immediate Stream Teardown:** Camera hardware tracks are explicitly closed and released (`MediaStreamTrack.stop()`) immediately when you pause the camera feed or navigate away from the practice studio component.

---

## 2. Privacy-Friendly Site Analytics
Signo utilizes **Vercel Web Analytics** for aggregate, cookieless, privacy-preserving performance monitoring (e.g. total pageviews, device country). Vercel Web Analytics **never collects cookies, personal identifiers, camera feeds, or biometric data**.

---

## 3. Local Browser Storage
Signo uses browser `localStorage` solely for non-sensitive local user preferences:
- Selected UI Language (`i18nextLng`)
- Mute/Audio state (`signo_muted`)
- Custom gesture calibration profiles (`gsl_pose_<LETTER>`)

Users may wipe this data at any time by clearing browser site storage.
