## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and privacy of Signo seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability or potential data exposure risk, **do NOT open a public GitHub issue**.

Please report vulnerabilities privately:

1. **Email:** Send details to `security@signo.dev` (or submit a Private Security Advisory via GitHub's "Security" tab).
2. **Details to Include:**
   - Description of the vulnerability or flaw.
   - Step-by-step instructions or proof-of-concept script.
   - Affected browser(s), operating systems, or package dependencies.
3. **Response SLA:**
   - **Initial Acknowledgement:** Within 48 hours.
   - **Triage & Assessment:** Within 5 business days.
   - **Patch & Advisory Release:** Targets resolution within 14 days of triage confirmation.

We appreciate responsible disclosure and will credit researchers in patch notes upon mutual agreement.
